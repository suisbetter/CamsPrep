CAMS Exam & Certification — Frequently Asked Questions

How hard is the CAMS exam and what is the pass rate?

The CAMS exam is challenging but very passable with focused preparation. It tests applied AML/CFT knowledge rather than memorization, and roughly 6 in 10 candidates pass on their first attempt — meaning about 4 in 10 do not. Most first-time failures come from under-estimating the scenario-based questions and relying only on the ACAMS study guide. Structured practice testing with realistic exam-style questions is the single biggest factor separating first-attempt passers from re-takers.

What is the passing score for the CAMS exam?

You need 75 marks out of 120, which is 62.5% — not 75% as many candidates wrongly assume. There is no partial credit, so you should answer every question because a blank guarantees zero while a guess still gives you a chance at the mark. Your pass or fail result appears on screen immediately after you finish the exam.

How many questions are on the CAMS exam and how long is it?

The CAMS exam has 120 questions and a time limit of 3.5 hours. It is a computer-based test delivered at Pearson VUE test centers (or via online proctoring). Alongside standard multiple-choice items, you will see multiple-selection questions that present five or six options and require you to choose two or three correct answers.

How long should I study for the CAMS exam?

Most successful candidates study for about 8 to 12 weeks, dedicating 6 to 10 hours per week, though this varies with your AML background. A proven approach is to read the ACAMS study guide once for coverage, then spend the majority of your time on chapter-wise and full-length practice tests to build exam stamina and expose weak areas. Reviewing detailed answer explanations for every question you miss is what turns study time into a first-attempt pass.

What are the eligibility requirements for the CAMS exam?

ACAMS uses a 40-credit eligibility system. Credits are earned through a mix of professional AML, compliance, or risk experience (18–24 months is typically recommended), relevant education such as a degree in finance, law, accounting, or criminal justice, and qualifying certifications or training. You document your experience and education when you apply, and you can check your standing using the official ACAMS eligibility calculator before you register.

How much does the CAMS certification cost?

The CAMS certification package for private-sector professionals is typically around US$2,095, which includes ACAMS membership, the official study materials, and the exam. Bundles that add a virtual classroom run higher (around US$2,495). A retake costs about US$299 and an exam extension around US$100. Because ACAMS updates its pricing periodically, always confirm the current fee on the ACAMS website before you register.

Is the CAMS certification worth it?

For anyone building a career in anti-money laundering or financial crime compliance, CAMS is the most widely recognized credential in the field and is frequently listed as preferred or required in AML job postings. Certified professionals generally command higher salaries and have stronger access to senior compliance roles, so the certification typically pays for itself well within the first year. It is most valuable when paired with hands-on compliance experience.

Can I retake the CAMS exam if I fail?

Yes. If you do not pass, you can retake the exam after a 30-day waiting period following your first attempt, and after 60 days following a second attempt. Each retake requires a fee of roughly US$299. Candidates who fail almost always improve by shifting their preparation toward timed, full-length mock exams rather than re-reading the study guide.

How long is the CAMS certification valid and how do I renew it?

CAMS certification is valid for three years. To recertify, you must maintain active ACAMS membership, earn the required continuing education credits over the three-year cycle, and pay a renewal fee (commonly in the US$200–$300 range depending on timing). Keeping a simple log of your CEU activities throughout the cycle makes renewal straightforward.

What is the best way to prepare for the CAMS exam?

The most effective preparation combines the official ACAMS study guide with heavy, realistic practice testing.
