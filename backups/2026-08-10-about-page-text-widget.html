About CAMS Prep

CAMS Prep is a specialized exam-preparation and professional learning platform dedicated to helping compliance professionals prepare for the Certified Anti-Money Laundering Specialist (CAMS) exam and strengthen the practical knowledge required for careers in anti-money laundering, financial crime prevention, and compliance.

 

Since 2021, CAMS Prep has directly supported more than 2,000 professionals through structured CAMS exam preparation, training programs, and learning resources.

 

Beyond our direct learners, CAMS Prep has built a growing global community of more than 20,000 compliance professionals across our learning platform, LinkedIn community, and social channels, where professionals engage with CAMS preparation resources, AML insights, and career-focused content.

 

Our mission is simple: to make CAMS preparation clearer, more focused, and more effective while helping professionals build knowledge that remains valuable beyond the exam.

 

We believe CAMS preparation should not be about consuming as much information as possible. It should help candidates understand core financial crime concepts, identify knowledge gaps, strengthen risk-based thinking, and prepare with greater structure and confidence.

 

Founded and Led by an Experienced Financial Crime Professional

CAMS Prep was founded by Rezaul Karim, CAMS, ICA, CCI, a senior compliance and financial crime risk professional with nearly a decade of experience across anti-money laundering, financial crime investigations, risk governance, regulatory compliance, and customer due diligence.

 

Most recently, Rezaul served as Assistant Vice President, Financial Crime Investigations at HSBC Bank, where his work included complex financial crime investigations involving AML, counter-terrorist financing, fraud, corruption, and related financial crime risks across multiple jurisdictions.

 

Throughout his career, he has contributed to high-impact initiatives designed to strengthen regulatory alignment, enhance governance, improve operational efficiency, and mitigate financial crime risk at scale. His experience includes financial crime investigations, transaction monitoring, customer due diligence, KYC, regulatory governance, compliance frameworks, operational risk management, and professional training.

 

Beyond his corporate career, Rezaul is a published author, international conference speaker, and active contributor to the global compliance community. His professional recognition includes ACAMS AML Professional of the Month in 2020, the HSBC Speak-Up Hero Award in 2021, the HSBC CEO Award in 2022, and recognition as an ACAMS Anti-Financial Crime Guru in 2024.

 

As Founder and Lead Trainer of CAMS Prep, Rezaul brings his practical industry experience into the platform's educational approach, helping learners connect CAMS exam concepts with the realities of AML, CFT, financial crime investigations, transaction monitoring, KYC/CDD, regulatory compliance, and risk management.

 

A Structured Approach to CAMS Exam Preparation

CAMS Prep provides learners with a structured, exam-focused learning ecosystem designed to support different stages of CAMS preparation.

Our resources include:

  • High-quality CAMS mock exams and practice questions
  • Chapter-wise and topic-focused CAMS tests
  • More than 800 CAMS flashcards for active recall and rapid revision
  • Structured study notes and practical explainers
  • Live, instructor-led CAMS preparation through the CAMS Prep Masterclass
  • Quizzes and knowledge checks designed to reinforce key concepts
  • Educational articles covering CAMS preparation, AML developments, financial crime trends, and compliance careers

Our learning resources are developed with a focus on clarity, relevance, and practical application. Rather than overwhelming candidates with unnecessary information, our goal is to help learners focus on what matters most, identify areas that require improvement, reinforce essential concepts, and approach the CAMS exam with greater confidence.

More Than Exam Preparation

At CAMS Prep, we believe professional development goes beyond passing an examination. Our broader goal is to help compliance professionals build strong foundations in anti-money laundering and financial crime prevention so they can apply their knowledge with confidence throughout their careers.

We aim to help professionals:

  • Build a stronger understanding of AML and financial crime concepts
  • Prepare for the CAMS exam with greater structure and confidence
  • Strengthen practical knowledge relevant to compliance and financial crime roles
  • Identify and close knowledge gaps through targeted practice
  • Prepare more effectively for compliance interviews and career opportunities
  • Stay informed about emerging financial crime risks and industry developments
  • Continue developing their professional capabilities beyond certification

Whether you are preparing for the CAMS exam for the first time, returning after a previous attempt, or looking to strengthen your broader financial crime compliance knowledge, CAMS Prep is designed to support you throughout your professional learning journey.

 

Built by Compliance Professionals, for Compliance Professionals

CAMS Prep's educational approach is shaped by professionals with practical experience across anti-money laundering, financial crime investigations, regulatory compliance, customer due diligence, transaction monitoring, governance, and risk management.

Our objective is to bridge the gap between exam preparation and real-world professional knowledge. Where appropriate, our educational content is informed by authoritative regulatory, industry, and professional sources and is reviewed to help maintain accuracy, relevance, and practical value.
