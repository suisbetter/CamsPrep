About CAMS PREP

CAMS PREP is a specialized exam-preparation and professional learning platform dedicated to helping compliance professionals prepare for the Certified Anti-Money Laundering Specialist (CAMS) exam and strengthen their practical knowledge of AML, CFT, and financial crime compliance.

Since 2021, CAMS PREP has directly supported more than 2,000 professionals through structured exam preparation, training programs, and learning resources.

Beyond our direct learners, CAMS PREP has built a growing global community of more than 20,000 compliance professionals across our learning platform, LinkedIn community, and social channels.
